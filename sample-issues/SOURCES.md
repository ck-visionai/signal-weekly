# Research notes for Career Weekly Sample Briefings

The following public career-services sources were reviewed on 24 August 2026. The sample briefings will paraphrase and cite their frameworks rather than reproducing their material.

| Source | Relevant use in the sample library |
|---|---|
| [MIT Career Advising & Professional Development — STAR method](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/the-star-method-for-behavioral-interviews/) | Supports the emphasis on specific, truthful examples; personal contribution; role relevance; flexible 3–5 story preparation; and reflection after interviews. |
| [University of Michigan Career Center — Interviewing Resources](https://careercenter.umich.edu/content/interviewing-resources) | Supports the Research, Reflect, Rehearse framing, role-and-organisation preparation, practical interview rehearsal and deliberate questions for employers. |
| [DePaul Career Center — Job Offers & Salary Negotiation](https://resources.depaul.edu/career-center/resumes-interviews/Pages/salary-negotiation.aspx) | Supports evaluating total compensation, researching market context, allowing time to consider an offer and recognising that negotiation is context-dependent. |
| [NYU Wasserman Center — Interviewing & Negotiation Process](https://wasserman.nyu.edu/channels/interviewing-negotiation-process/) | Confirms the practical relationship between interview preparation, offer evaluation and salary negotiation resources. |

The documents will make no guarantee of an interview, offer, salary increase or career outcome.
