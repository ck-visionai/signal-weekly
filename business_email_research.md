# Signal Weekly Business Email Research

Research checked 2026-08-18 from official provider pages.

| Option | Cost | Practical role | Key limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dedicated Gmail inbox | Free | Best immediate administrator inbox for signing up to Beehiiv and receiving account notifications | Uses an `@gmail.com` address, not a branded domain address |
| Zoho Mail Forever Free | Free in eligible regions | Custom-domain mailbox when the user owns a domain; one domain and up to five users | Availability is regional; IMAP, POP, and ActiveSync are not included |
| Cloudflare Email Routing | Free | Custom-domain receiving aliases that forward incoming mail to an existing inbox | Forwarding, not a full mailbox or general outbound sending service |

Official sources: https://support.google.com/mail/answer/9312312, https://www.zoho.com/mail/zohomail-pricing.html, https://www.cloudflare.com/products/email-routing/
