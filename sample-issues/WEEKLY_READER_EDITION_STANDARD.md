# Signal Weekly Weekly Reader-Edition Standard

## Purpose

Every scheduled **From the Sample Library** subscriber delivery must provide enough depth for a reader to spend approximately **20–30 minutes** reading, reflecting, and applying the material. This is a design target, not a guarantee: reading speed and the time a person chooses to spend on a worksheet will differ.

The public one-page PDFs remain **previews**. Sample Briefing 01 remains a short complete starter working page. The more substantial weekly materials must meet the standard below before they are linked in Beehiiv.

## Non-negotiable standard

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| Length | **8–12 finished PDF pages**, including relevant visual decision aids and worksheets. |
| Reader value | One clearly defined career decision, with explanation, examples, and a tool the reader can use. |
| Estimated engagement | Approximately **20–30 minutes** of reading and application. |
| Practicality | At least one worksheet, checklist, decision map, script, prompt set, or action plan. |
| Evidence and claims | Use practical career guidance; do not promise interviews, offers, salary outcomes, or employment results. |
| Editorial truth | Label each delivery **From the Sample Library** and **Sample Briefing**; never imply it was a historic newsletter issue. |
| Visual rhythm | Use visuals only when they clarify a decision, framework, example, or worksheet. Do not add decorative pages merely to increase length. |

## Recommended 10-page structure

An edition may be 8, 9, 10, 11, or 12 pages, but should follow the logical journey below.

| Page | Reader purpose | Required content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Orient | Title, who this is for, the career decision, and what the reader will complete. |
| 2 | Recognise the problem | A concise diagnosis of the common career moment or mistake. |
| 3 | Understand the model | A visual framework, decision map, or clear explanation of the underlying principle. |
| 4 | See it applied | A worked example, annotated scenario, or contrast between a weak and credible approach. |
| 5 | Prepare inputs | Prompts for the reader to collect their own evidence, context, or constraints. |
| 6 | Use the core tool | A worksheet, checklist, question bank, script, or structured template. |
| 7 | Make a decision | Trade-offs, red flags, alternatives, or a scoring/prioritisation aid. |
| 8 | Practise the language | A practical rehearsal prompt, message draft, interview answer, or conversation guide. |
| 9 | Commit to next steps | A 48-hour or seven-day action plan. Combine with Page 10 only when the edition is eight or nine pages. |
| 10 | Close well | A summary, honest scope note, and a reminder of the next weekly briefing. Extend with two more pages only when they contribute real reader value. |

## Page-count guide

| Edition length | Appropriate use |
|---|---|
| 8 pages | Narrow decision, one scenario, one primary tool. |
| 9–10 pages | Standard weekly edition; includes a visual framework, worked example, and worksheet. |
| 11–12 pages | More complex topic requiring multiple scenarios, a deeper decision map, or an extended action plan. |

## Production quality gate

Before scheduling a weekly delivery, the owner must confirm every item below.

- [ ] The edition has **8–12 finished pages** after final PDF export.
- [ ] The title and email clearly say **From the Sample Library** / **Sample Briefing**.
- [ ] The introduction names one specific career decision and the intended reader.
- [ ] The edition contains a practical visual, framework, or decision aid where it improves understanding.
- [ ] The edition contains at least one usable exercise or tool.
- [ ] A reader can identify a next step without being promised an outcome.
- [ ] The PDF has passed strict compilation, text verification, and visual review.
- [ ] The Beehiiv email uses the final approved PDF link, not a draft, preview, or earlier two-page outline.

## Required workflow for the current Sample Library

1. Retain the existing titles and one-page public previews.
2. Use the existing two-page PDFs only as internal source outlines.
3. Expand each Week 1–11 library topic into a new **8–12-page reader edition** using this standard.
4. Verify the final page count and content quality before uploading and adding its link to the Beehiiv send.
5. Send one extended edition with one current weekly Signal Weekly note; do not send the complete library at once.
