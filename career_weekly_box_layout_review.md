# Career Weekly Response-Area Layout Review

The representative 11-page Career Evidence Working Page and a representative 10-page reader edition were rendered in standard review mode after the width update.

The response areas now span the content column from left to right and remain aligned with the page margins. The Career Evidence document provides visibly usable rectangular writing spaces on the action pages; the reader edition uses the same full-width treatment on worksheet pages. No narrow vertical rectangles or obvious overflow were observed in the review sheets. The selected design is a full-width bordered writing area with a restrained grey rule and a short instructional hint where the document benefits from one.
