## Recommended email structure

Use a single email each week. Start with a short, current observation or practical career decision, then introduce the library edition with this standard wording:

> **From the Sample Library**
>
> This week’s working page is *[Title]*. It is a sample briefing edition prepared for practical reading—not a historic issue—and it includes one focused worksheet to use before your next career decision.

End with one simple action: **Download the complete working page**. Do not promise future editions are newly written unless they are. After Week 12, retire or refresh the sample library rather than recycling it indefinitely.
