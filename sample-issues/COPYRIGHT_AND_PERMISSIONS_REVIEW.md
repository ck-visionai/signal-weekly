# Signal Weekly Reader Library: Copyright and Permissions Review

> **Working review — not formal legal advice.** This is an operational copyright and permissions checklist for the current Signal Weekly reader-library assets. A qualified intellectual-property lawyer should review the library before relying on this assessment for registration, licensing, a dispute, or a high-volume commercial distribution decision.

## Review scope

This review covers the **twelve Sample Briefing topics**, including the one-page public previews, the Sample 01 starter working page, and the eleven 8–12-page subscriber reader-edition masters. It also covers the reports’ external reference links, the shared Typst document theme, and the need to document the provenance of website and PDF visual assets.

The U.S. Copyright Office explains that copyright protects independently created human expression fixed in a tangible medium, while it does not protect ideas, methods, systems, procedures or concepts. [1] The Office also notes that fair-use analysis is circumstance-specific, has no fixed “safe” word or percentage rule, and is ultimately determined by a court. [2]

## Distribution decision

### Suitable to prepare for delivery, subject to the release gate

The current reader editions are designed as original educational material. Their citations function as **further-reading links**, not as quoted or reproduced source content. The use of generic examples, original worksheets and Signal Weekly’s own layout reduces obvious third-party-content exposure.

However, this review does **not** certify that the PDFs are free of every copyright or rights-of-publicity issue. Copyright status depends on provenance and jurisdiction. The U.S. Copyright Office notes that, where ownership or permission is uncertain, seeking permission may be appropriate; the Office’s fair-use guidance does not provide a fixed quantitative safe harbour. [2]

## Review outcome

The **text-and-link review is complete** for the current reader-edition masters. The library is not cleared for full public distribution yet because the owner must still complete the visual-asset register and human editorial review described in the mandatory release gate.

## Operational rules for future issues

Use the following rules for every new Sample Briefing and weekly reader edition.

1. Write from an original outline and keep the editable source file.
2. Link to external resources for further reading; do not copy substantial passages, images or charts without a licence, permission or documented legal basis.
3. Treat every online image, logo, résumé, job description and screenshot as protected unless its permitted use is documented.
4. Keep an asset register and a source/version record for every released PDF.
5. Add a short educational-scope note, but do not treat a disclaimer as a substitute for permission where permission is required.
6. Before registering or commercialising the library at scale, obtain qualified legal advice about the relevant jurisdiction, contracts, authorship, AI-assisted components and brand assets.

The owner-facing [`ASSET_PROVENANCE_REGISTER.md`](./ASSET_PROVENANCE_REGISTER.md) lists the current project assets that need confirmation and provides the exact record format to retain.
