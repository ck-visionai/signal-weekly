# Mailing Address Notes for Signal Weekly

Research checked 2026-08-18.

Beehiiv states that an email footer must include an address and does not allow the address requirement to be removed through design customization. The United States FTC’s CAN-SPAM guidance states that commercial email must include a valid physical postal address; valid options include a current street address, a registered post-office box, or a private mailbox registered with an established commercial mail-receiving agency.

For Signal Weekly, the address requirement applies to newsletter **emails**. The website privacy-policy page can identify the business and provide a reliable contact method, but the same address can be shown there for transparency. A valid postal or commercial mailbox is a practical privacy-preserving alternative to a personal home address, provided it is legitimately registered and allowed for business correspondence.

Official sources: https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/6049111808279-how-to-customize-your-newsletter-footer and https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business
