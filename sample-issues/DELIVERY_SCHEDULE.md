# Signal Weekly: Twelve-Week Sample Library Delivery Schedule

This schedule is for **manual Beehiiv sending**. Each edition is accurately labelled **“From the Sample Library”**; it is not presented as a newly published historical newsletter issue. The Welcome Email delivers Sample Briefing 01 after confirmation. Then send **one weekly email**, combining a short current Signal Weekly note with the scheduled library edition so subscribers receive value without being overloaded by separate emails.

## Mandatory reader-edition standard

Every future weekly library delivery must be an **8–12-page reader edition**, designed for approximately **20–30 minutes of reading and application**. It must include a clear career decision, practical explanation, at least one worked example or scenario, a visual decision aid where useful, and a worksheet, checklist, or action plan. See [`WEEKLY_READER_EDITION_STANDARD.md`](./WEEKLY_READER_EDITION_STANDARD.md) for the required structure and quality gate.

> **Important:** The previously prepared two-page “full editions” are useful internal outlines, but they do **not** meet this new weekly reader-edition standard. Do not send their existing PDF links as weekly subscriber materials. Replace each with its expanded, verified 8–12-page edition before its scheduled delivery.

## Recommended email structure

Use a single email each week. Start with a short, current observation or practical career decision, then introduce the library edition with this standard wording:

> **From the Sample Library**
>
> This week’s reader edition is *[Title]*. It is a sample briefing edition prepared for practical reading—not a historic issue. Set aside 20–30 minutes to read the guidance, work through the scenario, and complete the decision tool before your next career move.

End with one simple action: **Open this week’s 8–12-page reader edition**. Do not promise future editions are newly written unless they are. After Week 12, retire or refresh the sample library rather than recycling it indefinitely.
