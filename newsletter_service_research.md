# Free Newsletter Service Research

Research checked 2026-08-18 from official pricing pages.

| Service | Free-plan allowance | Relevant included capabilities | Recommended use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beehiiv Launch | Up to 2,500 subscribers; unlimited email sends | Newsletter, website, subscribe forms, custom domains, campaign analytics | Best fit for a publication-style weekly newsletter with straightforward early-stage growth needs. |
| Kit Newsletter | Up to 1,000 subscribers; unlimited broadcasts, forms, and landing pages | One basic automation, subscriber tagging and segmentation, opt-in incentive delivery | Strong option if a welcome email or lead-magnet delivery is the first priority. |
| Mailchimp Free | Up to 250 contacts; up to 500 sends per month | One audience; basic early-growth email marketing | Least suitable for a weekly newsletter because the free limit is comparatively restrictive. |

Official sources: https://www.beehiiv.com/pricing, https://kit.com/pricing, https://mailchimp.com/pricing/marketing/
