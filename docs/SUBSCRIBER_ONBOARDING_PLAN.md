# Career Weekly Subscriber Onboarding Plan

## Launch position

Career Weekly uses a guided-library model. Every published edition remains previewable, Edition 01 remains the openly downloadable cornerstone working page, and selected past full editions are delivered by email during onboarding rather than exposed through an unrestricted public download bundle.

## Cadence

The catch-up journey uses no more than **two subscriber touchpoints per week**. The Friday edition remains the primary recurring touchpoint. The second touchpoint is a useful, situation-based recommendation rather than a generic promotional reminder.

| Touchpoint | Timing | Purpose | Primary action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start Here | Immediately after confirmation | Explain the promise, deliver Edition 01, and invite the reader to choose a situation. | Read the complimentary working page |
| Guided catch-up | One non-Friday day during the first full week | Send one relevant past full edition based on the reader’s selected situation. | Read the recommended briefing |
| Friday briefing | Next Friday and every Friday thereafter | Establish the normal weekly habit. | Read the new preview or briefing |

If the subscriber joins close to Friday, do not send two messages in a compressed window. Send Start Here immediately, allow the Friday briefing to serve as the week’s second touchpoint, and begin the guided catch-up recommendation the following week.

## Reader-facing promise

> New here? Start with the complimentary working page, then choose the career situation you want to work through. We’ll send the most relevant past briefings first, followed by the new edition every Friday.

## Situation labels

Use these four labels consistently in the archive and email recommendations: **Résumé Positioning**, **Executive Interviews**, **Career Decisions**, and **Offer Negotiation**. Each archive card should include a Start Here marker where appropriate, a “Most useful if…” sentence, an estimated reading time, a preview action, and one primary next action.

## Later capability

After the archive reaches approximately 12–20 strong editions, consider authenticated or email-gated **Subscriber Archive Access** for selected full editions one at a time. Do not enable unrestricted “Download All” access unless the product strategy changes and the access-control, sharing, and analytics implications are reviewed.
